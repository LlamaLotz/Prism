# Prism

Prism is a local-first personal knowledge workspace for students, researchers, and anyone working with their own notes and sources.

**Turn your own notes and sources into connected, searchable knowledge you control.**

Capture material, find it again, connect ideas, ask questions, and save what you learn. Markdown notes remain files you control. Optional AI uses your configured provider and existing privacy controls.

## Getting around

- **Notes** is the main workspace for notes, search, imports, and connections. The **AI assistant** remains directly accessible.
- **More** opens Graph, Topics, Split View, and Notebook. Existing keyboard shortcuts and native menu entries remain available.
- Notebook keeps Research, Search, and Chats prominent. **Tools** opens Transform, Podcasts, and Manage.
- AI settings keep privacy and your primary provider visible. **Advanced AI settings** contains per-feature routing and idle-model tuning.

New installations start in Notes and use 2D graphs by default. Existing saved preferences, features, notebooks, conversations, and data are preserved.

Prism is free and under active development. See [product scope](PRODUCT_SCOPE.md) for the focus and compatibility boundaries guiding further work.
