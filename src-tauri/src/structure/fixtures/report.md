QUARTERLY OPERATIONS REVIEW

Executive Summary

Revenue grew in every region except the coastal district. The coastal district lost one major customer and expects to recover next quarter. Operating margin improved as support costs fell.

Customer Support

Response time fell from nine hours to four hours. Staffing increased in the evening window. First-contact resolution improved after the knowledge base update.

Engineering

The platform migration completed without unplanned downtime. Two services still run on the old scheduler. The team will retire them in the next quarter.

Hiring

Six engineers joined and two contractors left. Recruiting time increased for senior roles. The training program now includes a local assistant for document review.

Risks

Supply delays could affect the hardware refresh. A key vendor contract expires at the end of the year. The security review found no critical issues.

Next Quarter

The team will finish the scheduler migration. Support will test a new triage model. Sales will open two regional pilot programs.
